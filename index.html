01 · Executive Summary

The kanto, given the setting it deserved.

Every Filipino who grew up in the 90s remembers the kanto: the sari-sari store bench, the Sunday-morning pandesal run, the barkada that gathered under a streetlight with nothing to do and nowhere better to be. That corner was where friendships were made. It was never comfortable, and it was never designed. It just worked.

Nuvìnta Cafe by Totoy Brown Tambayan takes that culture and gives it the setting it always deserved. It is an outdoor specialty coffee shop that pairs well-made espresso and thoughtful design with the sounds, flavors and objects of a 90s Filipino childhood. The name says it plainly: Nuvìnta means New Vintage.

What sets Nuvìnta apart is that the nostalgia is playable. Guests can race Mini 4WD cars on a Tamiya track, battle in Beyblade and Crush Gear arenas, shoot hoops on an arcade basketball machine, or settle into a retro lounge where Slam Dunk, Ghost Fighter and Flame of Recca play all day. They don't just look at the 90s. They play in it, stay longer, order more and come back with their barkada.

02 · Brand Identity & Concept

Where premium meets the kanto

The brand works because its two halves pull in opposite directions and meet in the middle. Nuvìnta sounds European, considered and premium; the accented ì signals craft. Totoy Brown is unmistakably local: the familiar nickname of the neighborhood kid, the color of barako coffee and pandesal crust. Together they promise elevated quality without pretension.

Pick a zone. Play the 90s.

Nuvìnta rebuilds those afternoons as dedicated play zones around the Bakuran, each sized for groups and designed to be watched as much as played.

The marks
